Featured System / Generator-Agnostic Detection

AI Video Detector

Can a model detect AI video by reasoning about artifacts, not fingerprints?

Most detectors fail when a new generator appears — they learned the fingerprint, not the artifact. This targets the harder, more durable problem: detecting AI-generated video through genuine spatial and temporal artifact reasoning, regardless of source.

  • Manually curated dataset: humans, animals, landscapes, real footage with AI modifications — designed to prevent shortcut learning
  • Grad-CAM and frequency analysis confirm genuine artifact attention — not generator-specific noise
  • Systematic ablation studies; architecture optimized for artifact-level reasoning
